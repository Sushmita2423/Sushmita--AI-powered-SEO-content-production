## 🛑 Who I Would NOT Recommend Following (And Why)

---

### ⚠️ Important Disclaimer
> **Please Note:** The observations below represent my personal perspective and strategic evaluation based on current search quality trends and my operational standards. This is **not** a personal criticism of any individual, creator, or practitioner. Every expert mentioned has built significant authority and contributed valuable insights to the digital marketing space. However, depending on your risk tolerance, business model, and brand goals, certain technical strategies may not align with a sustainable, long-term content strategy.

---

### 🚫 Craig Campbell

* **Why I Wouldn't Recommend Following for Most Brands:**  
  Craig Campbell is an absolute veteran in the industry and has always been transparent about his technical tactics. However, his core playbooks center heavily around aggressive, grey-hat automation setups—including deep database scraping, mass web scraping infrastructure, and automated link building to quickly monetize programmatic affiliate portals.

  If you are running a established brand, a corporate site, or a business focused on long-term authority, following these high-volume scraping methodologies carries severe operational risks. Modern search engines and AI scrapers actively target duplicate, scraped footprints during core quality updates. Building a sustainable business around these tactics often leads to sudden traffic drops and de-indexing, making it an unsafe roadmap for traditional growth teams.

---

### 🚫 Julian Goldie (Use With Extreme Caution)

* **Why I Wouldn't Recommend Following blindly:**  
  Julian Goldie provides incredible energy and fast-paced updates on AI agents and programmatic automation workflows. That said, his heavy push toward fully autonomous, rapid-deploy desktop workflows with minimal human oversight can easily lead beginners into a trap.

  Relying solely on hands-off AI agents to push high-volume content directly to live domains creates generic writing footprints, factual errors, and poor user engagement signals. While his methods are tempting for sheer speed, blindly adopting them without a strict human-in-the-loop editing process will likely result in long-term index penalties.
