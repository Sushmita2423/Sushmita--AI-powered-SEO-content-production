## ⚠️ Who I Would NOT Recommend Following and Why

---

### 🚫 Craig Campbell

* **Why I Cautiously Pass:**  
  Craig Campbell is undoubtedly an industry veteran who knows the technical mechanics of the web inside out, but his heavy emphasis on grey-hat database scraping, automated scraping setups, and aggressive link-building schemes is a dangerous strategy for anyone building a long-term brand. 

  While these ultra-aggressive tactics can sometimes trigger fast, temporary traffic spikes on churn-and-burn affiliate sites, they run directly counter to building sustainable organic equity. Modern search quality updates and AI web scrapers have gotten exceptionally good at identifying and de-indexing low-effort scraped data. For marketers and businesses looking to build durable search visibility that survives search engine updates, following this route introduces far more risk than reward.

---

### 🚫 Julian Goldie (For Pure Hands-Off Automation)

* **Why I Suggest Taking His Approach with Caution:**  
  Julian Goldie shares valuable frameworks on workflow speed and autonomous desktop agents, but relying on his push-button, fully automated programmatic builds without a strong human quality control layer is a trap for most creators. 

  Publishing fully automated, unedited AI content at scale usually results in generic writing footprints, factual errors, and poor user engagement signals. Unless you already have an advanced testing sandbox and a disposable domain portfolio, blindly copying hands-off automated publishing will likely lead to penalized domains and wasted indexation budgets.
